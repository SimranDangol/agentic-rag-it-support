# Lost Phone or Missing MFA Recovery Procedure — Acme Corp IT

## Overview
If you have lost, damaged, or upgraded your mobile device and cannot generate Microsoft Authenticator verification codes or receive push approvals, follow this emergency recovery procedure.

## Emergency Temporary Access Pass (TAP)

### What is a TAP?
A Temporary Access Pass (TAP) is a time-limited 8-digit alphanumeric passcode generated by the IT Helpdesk that satisfies MFA requirements for up to 8 hours, allowing you to log in and register a replacement device.

### How to Request a Temporary Access Pass
1. Contact the IT Helpdesk via phone:
   - Internal Extension: **ext. 4357**
   - External Helpline: **+1-800-555-4357**
2. Due to strict security protocols, you must pass identity verification:
   - Provide your Employee ID number.
   - Provide your manager's full name and corporate email.
   - A verification request will be dispatched to your direct manager via Slack or voice call.
3. Once approved, the technician will read your one-time TAP code over the phone or send it to an alternate verified personal email address.

## Re-registering Your New Phone

Once you have received your Temporary Access Pass:
1. On your computer, navigate to:
   `https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info`
2. Enter your email (`username@acme.example`).
3. When prompted for password or MFA, select **Use Temporary Access Pass instead** and enter the provided TAP.
4. On the Security Info dashboard, locate your old phone under methods and click **Delete** to revoke its security tokens.
5. Click **+ Add sign-in method** -> choose **Authenticator app**.
6. Install Microsoft Authenticator on your replacement device and scan the new QR code.
7. Test the new phone by approving the 2-digit number challenge.

## Stolen Device Protocol
If your smartphone was lost in a public location or stolen:
1. Notify IT Security immediately at `security@acme.example` or call **ext. 4357**.
2. The Helpdesk will immediately execute a **Revoke All Sessions** command in Entra ID, invalidating all current corporate tokens, and wipe corporate data if the device was enrolled in Microsoft Intune Mobile Device Management (MDM).
