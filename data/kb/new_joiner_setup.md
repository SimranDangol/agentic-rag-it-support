# New Joiner IT Onboarding & Laptop Setup — Acme Corp IT

## Welcome to Acme Corp!
This onboarding checklist guides new employees through initial hardware unboxing, account activation, security configuration, and mandatory software deployment.

## Step 1: Initial Laptop Unboxing & Power On
1. Unbox your company-issued Dell Latitude / Lenovo ThinkPad laptop and connect the USB-C 65W AC charger.
2. Power on the machine and wait for the **Windows Out-of-Box Experience (OOBE)** screen.
3. Connect the laptop to your home Wi-Fi or office network.
4. At the prompt "Sign in to your work or school account", enter your temporary username and password provided in your HR welcome email (`username@acme.example`).
5. Windows Autopilot will configure device encryption (BitLocker) and security certificates. This process takes 15–25 minutes.

## Step 2: Mandatory First-Day Configuration
1. **Set a Permanent Password**:
   - You will be forced to change your initial password on first login. Follow the 14-character complexity rules.
2. **Configure Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**:
   - When prompted with "More information required", download **Microsoft Authenticator** on your corporate or personal smartphone.
   - Scan the on-screen QR code and approve the test notification.
3. **Connect to GlobalProtect VPN**:
   - Locate the GlobalProtect icon in the system tray.
   - Portal address: `vpn.acme.example`.
   - Log in using your new credentials.

## Step 3: Software Installation via Acme Company Portal
Pre-approved core software is deployed automatically via Microsoft Intune. To install additional development or productivity tools:
1. Click the Windows Start button and launch **Company Portal**.
2. Select your needed software (e.g., Slack, Zoom, VS Code, Git, Google Chrome).
3. Click **Install**. Administrative credentials are not required for items in Company Portal.

## Step 4: Email and Collaboration Tools
1. Launch Microsoft Outlook. Your mailbox (`username@acme.example`) will automatically configure via modern authentication.
2. Launch Microsoft Teams and Slack to join your department channels.
