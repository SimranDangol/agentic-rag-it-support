# Scan-to-Email Setup & Usage Guide — Acme Corp IT

## Overview
All Acme Corp office Multi-Function Printers (MFPs) support high-resolution document scanning with automated delivery to your corporate inbox (`username@acme.example`).

## How to Scan a Document to Your Email

### Step 1: Authenticate at the Multi-Function Printer (MFP)
1. Approach any Ricoh or Canon MFP on your floor.
2. Tap your employee RFID/NFC badge on the card reader icon to authenticate.
3. Your name and Acme Corp email address will appear at the top of the touch screen.

### Step 2: Load Your Documents
- **Document Feeder (Multi-page documents)**: Place pages face up in the Automatic Document Feeder (ADF). Adjust the paper guides snugly.
- **Glass Platen (Books, receipts, ID cards)**: Lift the feeder lid and place the document face down on the glass, aligned with the top-left registration arrow.

### Step 3: Configure Scan Settings
1. On the MFP home screen, tap **Scan and Send** (or **Scan to My Email**).
2. The destination will default automatically to your email address (`username@acme.example`).
3. Tap **Options** to customize parameters:
   - **Color Mode**: Auto Color, Grayscale, or Black & White.
   - **Resolution**: 300 DPI (recommended for documents) or 600 DPI (for detailed diagrams/photos).
   - **File Format**: Compact PDF (default), Searchable PDF (with OCR), or TIFF.
   - **2-Sided Original**: Enable if your paper originals are double-sided.
4. Press the green **Start** button on the physical control panel.

## Troubleshooting Scan-to-Email Delivery

### 1. Attachment File Size Limit
- Corporate email security enforces a maximum attachment size of **25 MB**.
- If scanning large packets (50+ pages), split the batch into smaller jobs or reduce resolution to **200 DPI Grayscale** in scan settings.

### 2. Email Not Arriving
- Scanned documents typically arrive within 1 to 3 minutes from sender `scanner-daemon@acme.example`.
- Check your **Junk Email** folder in Outlook.
- Verify that your corporate mailbox is not full (see `mailbox_full.md`).

### 3. OCR (Searchable Text) Requirements
If you require searchable text, select **Searchable PDF** in file format settings before pressing Start.

For badge reader issues or printer hardware maintenance, call IT Helpdesk at **ext. 4357**.
